Privacy Policy for Dorm to Studio

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Dorm to Studio ("the game") is published by NikStudio. This page explains what data the game and the services it uses collect.

Data the game itself keeps

The game has no accounts and does not send your progress to our servers. Your save and your settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Third-party services

Google AdMob shows optional rewarded ads. It may collect the advertising ID, device information, approximate location (from the IP address) and data about how you interact with ads, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Google Play Billing processes in-app purchases. We never receive your payment details.

Your choices

In regions where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice in the game settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
